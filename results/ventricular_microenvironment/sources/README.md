# Sources for ventricular microenvironment hypotheses

The source article, *Spatially guided in vivo single-cell functional genomics of postnatal heart* (DOI [10.1038/s44161-026-00861-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44161-026-00861-z)), provides direct postnatal spatial evidence for **Slit2.CM trabecular enrichment** and **Ankrd1.CM left-ventricular/peri-arteriolar enrichment**. This is more directly relevant to these data than applying embryonic compact/trabecular markers alone.

- `paper_supplementary_table3_CM_markers.tsv`: exact factual gene/statistic rows for five CM states in author Supplementary Table3 (top10 supplied per state). Author p-values are provenance only, not reanalysis results. Table3 is not stage-specific.
- `paper_provenance.json`: retrieval links, workbook/sheet, short exact attributed excerpts, regional claims and limits. Full article and supplement are not republished.
- `curated_programs_and_limitations.json`: exact paper marker sets and smaller analyst-curated oxygen/metabolic/mechanocoupling/control panels. Curated sets are hypotheses rather than validated spatial or physiological assays.
- `supporting_references.json`: primary/curated references and Hallmark source mirror.

Slit2-associated, Gja5/Etv1-associated, trabecular and conduction programs overlap. A conduction-associated label must not automatically exclude Slit2-rich nuclei from an analysis of ventricular wall heterogeneity. Spatial identity remains unverified in our snRNA nuclei. Hypoxia-response RNA, glycolysis, nuclear OXPHOS and structural-coupling expression should be evaluated separately; none directly measures oxygen concentration, metabolic flux, force or strain.
