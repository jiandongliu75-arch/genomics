# Paper-guided analysis decisions

## Lu et al. 2021: distinguish ancestry, growth, and feedback

Full title: *Perinatal angiogenesis from pre-existing coronary vessels via DLL4–NOTCH1 signalling*. Nature Cell Biology23,967–977. DOI [10.1038/s41556-021-00747-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41556-021-00747-1); PMID34497373. Full uploaded PDF,13 pages, was read, including results, figures and methods. Figures3 and6 were also inspected visually.

1. **Origin is experimentally traced.** Npr3 endocardial and Bmx coronary lineage labeling at late fetal/neonatal times distinguishes two populations. The Npr3-2A-CreERT2 design preserves the Npr3 protein rather than making a null allele. The result concerns the labeling windows tested: it does not negate embryonic endocardial contributions earlier in development. Expression similarity or optimal transport cannot replace this lineage evidence.
2. **The expanding coronary plexus is regional.** Inner myocardial coronary vessels are relatively Flt4/VEGFR3 and Dll4 enriched. Aplnr/Emcn show a complementary outer/venous-compatible pattern; Emcn also marks endocardial populations. Neither Flt4 nor Emcn alone gives a unique vessel identity. In our data Emcn is not in the measured Xenium403-gene panel. Flt4 alone cannot identify lymphatics.
3. **Angiogenesis was measured beyond RNA.** Regional Hypoxyprobe, EdU incorporation, vessel protrusions/branching and inner/outer myocardial density were used. EdU was administered2h and Hypoxyprobe90min before sampling. Quantification used3–6 independent hearts and blinded analysis. RNA programs can nominate corresponding states, not measure perfusion or recreate these experiments.
4. **Direction of Notch perturbation matters.** Dll4 deletion induced at P2 enhances vascular/VEGFR3 expansion at P5. NICD activation at P1 suppresses growth, with reduced Dll4/Flt4 at P7. Thus DLL4 marks the growth-associated plexus while DLL4/NOTCH provides feedback restraint. A positive sum of pathway transcripts cannot be assumed to increase with sprouting output.
5. **The developmental window is fast.** The authors describe inner/outer vascular density convergence by P7 and waning activity approximately P7–P14, with diminished VEGFR3 at P14. Our four sections miss fetal and P1–P6 intervals. P1 apical resection tracing supports pre-existing coronary revascularization in that injury model; it is not a test of normal CM maturation.

Implemented: separate growth-compatible, Notch-response, cycling, outer/venous-compatible, endocardial and lymphatic-compatible readouts; audit full-RNA detection/coherence; treat Flt4+Dll4 co-detection as a measured proxy within a QC-defined population; prevent endocardial-to-coronary lineage inference. The growth proxy excludes profiles *assigned* endocardial or lymphatic nuclear identity; unresolved subtype is not proof that those alternatives are absent.

## Statistical and geometric choices in this extension

- All original408,054 profile IDs, native coordinates, broad labels and original count files remain unchanged. Review profiles remain visible in audit/background data.
- The primary eligible EC source definition is inherited from the validated v1 atlas. Source selection, nuclear subtype uncertainty, public labels and capture depth are exposed separately. Missing nuclear area does not alone exclude a CM.
- Native boundary graphs use exact Shapely polygon distance≤0.5/1/2µm. Refined contact means an operational shared territory boundary, not verified plasma membrane contact. The50µm graph inherits tissue-gap screening from v1.
- Initial all-eligible models compare technical, anatomical, CM-state and receiver-conditioned adjustments. The same-CM geometry sensitivity additionally adjusts neighboring EC nuclear/core capture and eligible/all-broad source coverage. Models include joint EC programs/families, CM sender/receiver, other-cell composition, focal capture/area, density, spatial splines and anatomical distance proxies. Block bootstrap uses400µm blocks,256 draws, inherited seed731. These are exploratory conditional associations; no multiple-testing claim or biological replicate inference is made.
- Source-definition sensitivity changes the PTPRM sender population while holding the other primary EC exposure variables fixed, on identical CMs. It does not validate public labels or compare two fully refitted communication networks.
- Whole-RNA programs use corrected full-transcriptome counts and the full corrected library size. Duplicate symbol mappings are summed. Eight library profiles are shown rather than testing a correlation across four age means. Module coherence adjusts library-internal EC family and count depth and requires enough detected genes; sparse or opposing feedback genes need not form a positive module.
- We retain native/core/CM-control/all-cell-control score distinctions. Core RNA reduces one boundary-assignment sensitivity; it does not eliminate cytoplasmic contamination or establish a nucleus for every CM section.

## Wormhole decision and temporal limits

The learned metric is assessed before it is used for biological summaries. Adoption requires held-out within-age Sinkhorn Spearman≥0.8 and no more than0.05 below the mean-state baseline in **every** age. Only P7 passes; general adoption is rejected. Same-CM spatial phenotype prediction also shows little added value. This is an assessment of this CPU configuration/feature representation, not a claim that Wormhole cannot work on richer datasets or with further training.

The fallback six-state-per-age mean-feature archetypes and exact medoid OT costs are descriptive data summaries. FixedK6 is not seed-stability-selected. A reciprocal nearest medoid identifies similar observed neighborhoods across sections; it is neither an ancestor/descendant relation nor a calibrated dynamical process. No proliferation-based growth mass, fetal observations or tracked cells are available to support a real developmental transport flow.

The next experiment should jointly resolve anatomy, vascular growth/perfusion, EC–CM contact and CM maturation in independent hearts, with an EC-directed perturbation when testing EC sender necessity. Additional algorithms cannot supply those missing observations.
