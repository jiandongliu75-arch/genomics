# A functional experiment selected from the spatial results

## Hypothesis and reason for priority

**The CM phenotype associated with capillary apposition depends on developmental state.** At P7, strict local matches show a lower-cycling RNA contrast; P14/P21 show metabolic-regulatory contrasts. Geometry alternatives support some associations, while strict capillary-source definitions attenuate them. These patterns do not establish a causal switch, and low late cycling constrains the withdrawal comparison.

The first experiment should distinguish oxygen-dependent support from physical EC-contact effects across CM states. Starting with an EC PTPRM knockout alone could conflate an instructive interaction with altered EC health or support, and assumes a mechanism the observational analysis does not establish.

## Factorial design

| Factor | Comparison | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Initial CM state | Developmentally early versus later preparations; characterize baseline state and viability | Tests whether the CM response differs with developmental competence |
| EC contact | Direct EC–CM coculture versus physically separated ECs with matched medium and EC number | Tests the added effect of apposition beyond diffusible support |
| Oxygen availability | Two measured, noninjurious conditions selected by pilot | Tests oxygen dependence while avoiding injury-associated arrest |

Hold matrix composition/mechanics, EC density, culture duration and medium comparable. Include CM-only controls and randomize treatments within each donor preparation. Use the same initial EC source across the CM-state comparison initially, so different EC ages do not become an uncontrolled explanation. EC developmental state can be crossed subsequently if an effect is established.

Use at least four independent donor preparations per initial CM state in the pilot, with technical wells nested within donor. Use between-donor variance to power the confirmatory study. Do not power it from tens of thousands of spatial cells or call wells independent hearts. The practical CM preparation/culture system must preserve comparable viability and baseline state; nominal culture days alone do not establish equivalence to postnatal age.

## Endpoints

Primary functional readouts are mitochondrial respiration/substrate use and calcium-transient/contractile performance. Secondary readouts include sarcomere organization, CM growth, cycling/withdrawal, ploidy and corresponding RNA programs. EdU or cycling transcripts alone do not demonstrate productive division or completed maturation.

Measure oxygen and monitor survival, ROS and DNA-damage responses. A treatment that arrests cells through injury should not be interpreted as promoting maturation. Quantify actual EC–CM interfaces rather than assuming that equal cell number gives equal contact.

## Prespecified interpretation

| Result | Implication and next action |
|---|---|
| Oxygen affects metabolic/function endpoints; direct contact adds little at matched conditions | Prioritize permissive delivery support. Verify the in vivo correlate using perfused-vessel labeling and regional hypoxia measurements. |
| Contact improves a CM endpoint at matched oxygen and diffusible conditions | Prioritize an instructive/interface mechanism. Next test EC-specific Ptprm loss and rescue with WT CMs, with EC viability/support and contact extent verified. |
| CM state modifies the contact response | Supports the developmental-competence hypothesis; relate response-specific RNA programs to the observed P7 versus later windows. |
| EC PTPRM loss removes a contact benefit, and rescue restores it | Supports EC-specific necessity in the tested system. Reciprocal CM perturbation distinguishes partner requirements. MEIS1 rescue tests mediation of a cell-cycle effect; it is not assumed in advance. |
| Controlled oxygen/contact effects do not reproduce | Reconsider shared regional development, load, other trophic cues or matrix context. Do not infer a ligand–receptor mechanism from proximity alone. |

Keep matrix constant initially. Present apposition-by-FB basement-program interactions are not consistently positive, and transcript expression is not deposited ECM or stiffness. A separate matrix-by-contact experiment is justified if direct matrix measurements or replicated data identify a modifier.

## Replication and missing ages

The existing spatial series has one available section per measured age. P3/P5/P10 transcript expectations were computed from observed RNA endpoints and model choice; they are not new biological samples. An in vivo validation series should prioritize the early expansion interval and the P7–P14 transition, with independent hearts and anatomically matched sections. Perfused-vessel/hypoxia imaging would test a physiological link absent from the current matrices.

This document is an experiment-ready analytical specification, not a claim that animal, culture, perfusion or functional assays have been performed.
