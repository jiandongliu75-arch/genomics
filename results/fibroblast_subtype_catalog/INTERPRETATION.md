# Fibroblast markers, candidate functions and spatial evidence

The current hierarchy contains **five broad RNA families, multiple within-age substates, and a cycling overlay**. The focused review places P21_F0 in the broader Col8a1-related family while retaining its distinct Adgrl3/Svep1 substate. This improves the RNA interpretation; it does not establish the anatomy of every group.

**The Xenium refit is now complete:** [updated maps, clean RNA figures, boundary confidence and spatial models](../fibroblast_Xenium_reviewed_refit/README.md). Supported P21 fine labels increase from 197 to 1,497/13,083 profiles (11.4%). The five residual-lineage groups are excluded from clean fibroblast analysis, with their source/QC records preserved.

## Broad families

All identities assume a coherent fibroblast-lineage context (for example Dcn/Lum/Pdgfra/Tcf21 and matrix genes), rather than a single positive marker. Gene panels overlap. Listed functions describe RNA-supported programs or hypotheses, not measured protein deposition, cell behavior or mechanics.

| Family/state | Representative identity or associated markers | Stages with a named RNA group | Functional reading | Current spatial evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Postn-associated developmental fibroblasts | Postn, Tnc, Col24a1, Ptn; substates include Sntg1/Slit3, Eln, laminins, Fn1/Frem1 | P0, P7 | Developmental matrix production/remodeling, adhesion and growth-factor-associated programs. Within the family, P0→P7 increases in Col3a1/Lamc1/Postn accompany decreases in Ptn/Wt1/Tbx18. This is developmental remodeling, not a diagnosis of pathological fibrosis. | Best-supported fine family in early sections: 1,591 P0 and 5,167 P7 profiles pass the fine-label rules. Family-weighted neighborhoods contain many CMs and ECs, consistent with a myocardial/interstitial context; not an exclusive anatomical compartment. |
| Col8a1-associated interstitial fibroblasts | Col8a1, Col15a1, Htra3, Hmcn2, Cmklr1, Col6a3; Adgrl3/Svep1/Fap versus Hmcn2/Pcsk6 substates | P14, P21 | Matrix organization and relatively higher basement-membrane RNA than the Gfpt2 family at both late ages. The broader reviewed P21_F0+F2 grouping preserves this comparison. Matrix adhesion/processing specializations remain hypotheses. | Reviewed-reference family weights have CM/EC-containing environments. The completed refit supports 1,390 P21 Col8a1-related profiles, but does not establish a uniquely peri-arterial or mural-rich compartment. |
| Gfpt2-associated fibroblasts | Gfpt2, Adgrd1, Cmah, Sema3c, Scara5; paper panel also includes Pi16 and Has2 | P7, P14, P21 | Metabolic/glycosylation-related and matrix-organizing program. At P14/P21, relatively higher fibronectin and elastic-fiber-associated RNA than the original Col8a1 groups. Gfpt2/Has2 expression alone does not establish hyaluronan production or matrix stiffness. | Family-weighted environments include CMs, ECs and pericytes; resemble Col8a1 environments more than a sharply separate compartment. Fine spatial support is limited, especially at P21. No demonstrated epicardial, perivascular or transmural restriction. |
| Lsamp-associated fibroblasts | Lsamp, Eln, Cfh, Slit3, Itgbl1; some groups also express Col8a1. P21_F5 additionally has Col4a5/Col4a6 enrichment | P14, P21 | Elastic-matrix and adhesion/signaling-associated programs. F1 and F5 differ transcriptionally within this family; collagen-IV-associated RNA distinguishes F5. Neither matrix elasticity nor vascular-support activity has been functionally measured. | Soft family weights favor fibroblast-rich neighborhoods with fewer nearby CMs than Col8a1/Gfpt2 weights. Some P14 mural association is descriptive and not consistent enough across ages to define a niche. Eln makes an outflow/great-vessel relationship plausible, but an outflow-tract location is unverified. Existing transfer resolves family proposals, not separate F1/F5 locations. |
| Kirrel3-associated fibroblasts | Kirrel3, Erbb4, Unc5c, Abi3bp, Kcnq5; paper's valve-family panel includes Rspo3, Lef1, Ltbp2 | P0, P7, P14, P21 | Distinct adhesion/signaling-associated RNA program, resembling the paper's valve-associated family. Valve maintenance/function is an anatomical hypothesis rather than a result of this analysis. | Soft proposals favor fibroblast-dense and CM-poor territories. Only Kirrel3 of the paper's top-ten valve markers is measured by Xenium, so no profiles pass our two-marker fine gate for this family. These territories cannot currently be called valves. |
| Cycling-associated fibroblasts (overlay) | Top2a, Diaph3, Rad51b, Pola1; broader cell-cycle panel includes Mki67/Cenpf | P0_F5, P7_F7; small P14_F7 candidate | DNA replication/cell-cycle activity superimposed on an underlying fibroblast identity. Completed cell division is not demonstrated. | No independently validated subtype-specific niche. P14_F7 contains only 35 nuclei and remains family-unresolved. |

Absence of an exclusive family label at an age does not mean that its marker expression is absent. Counts are QC-retained nuclei, not unbiased tissue abundances. F numbers are stage-local: P0_F1 and P21_F1 are not automatically the same subtype.

## What the spatial measurements actually show

The following values describe **soft family-weighted** 30-µm neighborhoods in the reviewed transfer, restricted to profiles with at least five resolved neighbors and finite arterial-distance measurement. They are not percentages of fibroblasts assigned to an anatomical compartment. Soft weights are not calibrated identity probabilities. Numbers are descriptive within one section per age.

| Stage/family | CM share of resolved neighbors | FB share | EC share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| P0 Postn | 54.1% | 22.2% | 16.2% |
| P7 Postn | 44.6% | 32.1% | 16.3% |
| P14 Col8a1 | 44.1% | 20.3% | 22.7% |
| P14 Gfpt2 | 45.8% | 18.9% | 23.0% |
| P14 Lsamp | 35.1% | 30.1% | 18.3% |
| P21 Col8a1 (reviewed F0+F2 reference) | 46.4% | 24.0% | 20.0% |
| P21 Gfpt2 | 47.7% | 22.9% | 19.6% |
| P21 Lsamp | 37.2% | 36.1% | 17.2% |
| P21 Kirrel3 proposal only | 11.3% | 71.3% | 9.9% |

Thus the most defensible descriptive contrast is **CM/EC-containing neighborhoods for Col8a1/Gfpt2 programs versus more fibroblast-rich neighborhoods for Lsamp and Kirrel3 proposals**. This does not identify valves, outflow tracts, epicardium, cell contact or directional signaling. Different section planes and tissue composition prevent interpreting these numbers as replicated age effects.

The fraction of broad spatial fibroblasts passing the unchanged fine-label rules is 68.6% at P0, 64.3% at P7, 13.7% at P14 and 11.4% at P21 after the completed refit (previously 1.5% at P21). These strict rules trade sensitivity for consistency. Late-stage family and especially substate maps remain incomplete; the reviewed-reference maps and boundary tests are available in the linked refit report.

No fibroblast subtype currently defines a secure exclusive niche around Ankrd1-associated CMs. Native-boundary matrix associations weaken or change with segmentation alternatives. Fibroblast features also add little held-out prediction of the 21-gene CM maturation score after the existing spatial/cell-composition baseline. These observations do not exclude fibroblast effects through unmeasured signals, deposited matrix or earlier exposures.
